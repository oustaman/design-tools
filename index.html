Design tools

Small tools for UX and service design work. Each one makes a 16:9 slide you can copy into a deck.

  • Flow makerA journey as it is now and as you would change it, with a table of what happened to every step.
  • Blueprint makerA service blueprint: what people do, what they see, what happens behind it, and where it goes wrong.
  • Screen makerWireframes for phone or desktop, built from ready-made pieces, with numbered notes.
  • 2×2 makerRiskiest assumptions, value against effort, stakeholders, or your own axes.
  • Plan makerPhases against what you do, what you need to learn, outcomes, measures and risks.
  • Decision makerFrom a problem and its evidence to the option you chose, what it costs, how you will test it and how you will know it worked.
Your work stays in your browser. Each tool's Save button gives you text to keep and paste back later.
