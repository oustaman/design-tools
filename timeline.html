How it works

  • Events go along the line in the order you list them. Change the order with ↑ and ↓.
  • Spacing has three settings. Evenly gives every event the same room. By the dates or numbers in When spaces them as time passed, so a long wait shows as a long gap: it reads “2025-03”, “March 2025”, “5 June 2026”, “Q2 2025”, “2026”, month names on their own, and numbers such as “Week 3” or “Day 10”, as long as every When uses the same kind. Free leaves each event where you drag it.
  • Drag a dot along the line to place an event by hand, or select it and use ← and → (Shift for bigger steps). The first move switches the spacing to Free and keeps everything else where it was.
  • Time marks put a tick on the line at a set interval: every week, 2 weeks, month, 2 months, quarter, 6 months, year or 5 years, or every 1, 2, 5… for numbers such as “Week 3”. Auto picks the finest interval that gives no more than 14 marks. When labels would touch, only every second or third one is written, and every tick stays.
  • Time marks need time behind the line: spacing by date, or Free with a start and end. Moving from By date to Free keeps the dates as the start and end, so the marks stay put.
  • Side puts an event’s card above or below the line. Auto takes turns along the line. Cards that would overlap stack outwards instead.
  • Key marks a milestone: a diamond on the line and a stronger card.
  • Phases shade a run of events, from one event to another: Discovery, Alpha, Beta, Live, or your own.
  • Move a phase by dragging its shading or its name; it keeps its width. A click on the name, without moving, still lets you rename it. With the name selected, ← and → move it too.
  • Resize a phase by dragging the small bar at either edge, or select the bar and use ← and → (Shift for bigger steps). Phases can then overlap, leave gaps, or start before the first event. Fit to the events puts it back, and so does choosing From or To again.
  • Nothing is cut off. The text gets smaller until everything fits, and the tool says when it is too small to read on a call. Fewer events, or shorter words, keep it large.
  • Copy the slide puts a 16:9 picture on the clipboard. If the browser refuses, it downloads.
