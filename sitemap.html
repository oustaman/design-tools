Enter adds a page below. Tab moves a page under the one above, and Shift and Tab moves it back out.

    Click any words on the slide to change them.

    How it works

    • Type the pages as an outline. The first line is the top of the site, and the tree on the slide is drawn from the outline.
    • Enter adds a page below. Tab moves a page under the one above, and Shift and Tab moves it back out. Pages under it go with it.
    • Click a page on the slide to rename it there. Enter adds a page below, Tab moves to the next, and Esc finishes.
    • ↑ and ↓ move a page, and the pages under it, past its neighbours.
    • Showing a change? Mark a page new, changed, moved or removed, and the word appears on its box, so it still reads in black and white.
    • Nothing is cut off. The text gets smaller until everything fits, and the tool says when it is too small to read on a call.
    • Copy the slide puts a 16:9 picture on the clipboard. If the browser refuses, it downloads.

    Your work stays in this browser: press Save to keep it as text and carry on anywhere.
