    How it works

    • Pick a board: riskiest assumptions, value against effort, stakeholders, or your own. Each keeps its own notes.
    • Add notes on the right. Long notes wrap and are never cut off.
    • Place them by dragging, or select one and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps).
    • Axes and corners are yours to rename.
    • On the slide, click the title, a corner or an axis to change its words. Click a selected note again to change its words, or press Enter on it.
    • Copy the slide puts a 16:9 picture on the clipboard. If the browser refuses, it downloads.

    Your work stays in this browser: press Save to keep it as text and carry on anywhere.
