Canvas maker

More

Click any words on the slide to change them.

How it works

  • Pick a canvas. The fields and their layout change to suit it, and each canvas keeps its own words when you switch.
  • Each field has a question to help you think. The words are yours: type one note per line, and each line becomes its own note on the slide.
  • Click any field on the slide to change its words there. Enter starts a new note, Tab moves to the next field, and Esc finishes.
  • Show the questions on the slide to print a blank worksheet for a workshop or a class.
  • Nothing is cut off. The text gets smaller until everything fits, and the tool says when it is too small to read on a call.
  • Copy the slide puts a 16:9 picture on the clipboard. If the browser refuses, it downloads.

Your work stays in this browser: press Save to keep it as text and carry on anywhere.
