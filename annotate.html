    How it works

    • Add a picture: paste a screenshot, drop a file on the slide, or choose one. A photo of a letter, a form or a sign works as well as a screen.
    • Click the picture where a note belongs. A numbered marker goes there and the note opens for you to type.
    • Say three things in each note: what is there, who it affects, and why it matters. “Confusing” on its own tells nobody anything.
    • Move a marker by dragging it, or select it and use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps).
    • Click a note on the slide to change its words there. Tab moves to the next, and Esc finishes.
    • Nothing is cut off. The notes get smaller until they fit beside the picture, and the tool says when they are too small to read on a call.
    • Copy the slide puts a 16:9 picture on the clipboard. If the browser refuses, it downloads.

    Your work stays in this browser, picture included: press Save to keep it as text and carry on anywhere. The text is long when it holds a picture.
